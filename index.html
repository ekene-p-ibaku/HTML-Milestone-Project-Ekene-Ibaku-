How Planetary Data Helps Us Understand Space

Every planet in our solar system tells a unique story. By studying planetary data, including mass, orbital period, distance from the sun, and atmospheric composition, we can gain deep insights into how planets form, evolve, and sometimes develop the conditions necessary for life. Space data makes the invisible visible.
